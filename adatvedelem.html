Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Az adatvédelmi nyilatkozat angol nyelvű, és szó szerint megegyezik az appban olvasható változattal.

1. Introduction

Fade Lab ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of salon owners, barbers, staff, and their clients who use our application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains who we are, what personal data we collect, why and on what legal basis, who we share it with, and what rights you have regarding your information.

2. Who We Are (Data Controller)

The App is developed and operated by Balog Dávid, an individual developer based in Budapest, Hungary. He is the "data controller" responsible for your personal data under this Policy. If the App is later operated through a registered business, this section will be updated with its details. The contact details in Section 17 are the correct way to reach us about any privacy matter.

3. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Account information: your name, your email address and password (used only for sign-in via Supabase Authentication), and your role (salon owner, staff, or client). If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us so we can create and sign in to your account — we never see your Google password. When you book as a client, your name appears on the booking so the salon knows who is coming.

Salon business data: salon name, opening hours, service listings and pricing, and an optional logo image.

Staff data: name, an optional profile photo, working hours, breaks, vacation dates, and — for staff invited by email — the invited email address.

Client records: when a salon adds or books a client, we store that client's name, phone number, notes, visit count, total spend, and any outstanding balance. These records are managed by the salon, not by the client directly, unless the client books through the App with their own account.

Booking data: appointment date and time, selected service, assigned staff member, guest name and phone number, notes, payment method (cash/card/unpaid), and status (confirmed, arrived, no-show, cancelled).

Push notification token: if you allow notifications, we store a device push token (via Expo/Apple/Google push services) so the salon and its clients can notify each other about booking updates.

Subscription status: whether a salon's trial or subscription is active, and its renewal date. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe, inside a secure payment page — Fade Lab never sees or stores your card number.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

Performance of a contract: account data, salon/staff/client records, and booking data, because we cannot provide the booking service without them.

Legitimate interests: keeping the App secure, preventing abuse, and improving reliability (e.g. offline caching, crash prevention) — always balanced against your right to privacy.

Consent: device permissions you actively grant (camera, photo library, calendar, notifications) — you can withdraw this consent at any time in your device settings, which stops the related processing going forward.

Legal obligation: retaining certain billing records where tax or accounting law requires it.

5. Device Permissions

Depending on how you use the App, we may ask for the following device permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

Photo library / camera: to let salon owners choose a salon logo and staff photos.

Calendar: with your explicit permission, the App can add, update, or remove your bookings as events in your phone's own calendar app, so they show up alongside your other appointments. This uses your device's built-in calendar (which may itself be backed by a Google or Apple account you've set up on your phone) — Fade Lab does not connect to your Google or Apple account directly, and you can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Notifications: to send booking confirmations, reminders, and status updates.

On iOS, the App also declares the standard App Tracking Transparency permission string as required by Apple; Fade Lab does not currently use any advertising or cross-app tracking SDK, so this permission has no practical effect today.

6. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to: (a) provide and maintain the booking and salon management service; (b) process appointments and send booking confirmations and reminders (via push notification and, optionally, your device calendar); (c) manage your subscription and billing through Stripe; (d) let salon owners generate revenue and booking reports — these reports are generated on your own device and shared only through your device's native share sheet (e.g. Mail, Messages), Fade Lab does not upload report files to any additional server; (e) let you keep viewing and creating bookings while offline, by temporarily caching your salon's data on your device and syncing any changes once you're back online; (f) improve the App's functionality; (g) communicate with you about service updates, changes, or issues; (h) comply with legal obligations. We do not use your personal data for any purpose incompatible with these.

7. Data Sharing & Sub-processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, and we do not share it for third-party advertising. We share data only with the service providers ("sub-processors") needed to run the App: (a) Stripe, for subscription billing and payment processing; (b) Supabase, our database, authentication, and file storage provider; (c) Expo, Apple, and Google, whose push notification infrastructure delivers notifications to your device; (d) Google, as a sign-in provider, if you choose "Continue with Google"; (e) law enforcement or regulatory authorities, only when required by law. Salon owners and their staff can see the booking and contact details (name, phone number) of clients who book at their salon — this access is scoped to that salon's own clients only and is necessary for the salon to provide its services. We do not currently use any additional analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting sub-processors beyond those listed here; if that changes, we will update this section.

8. International Data Transfers

Our sub-processors (Supabase, Stripe, Expo/Apple/Google) may process data on servers located outside your own country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer for international transfers (such as standard contractual clauses), as required under GDPR and similar laws.

9. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase (hosted on top of PostgreSQL), which provides encrypted data storage and secure authentication. Database access is restricted with row-level security rules so that a salon's business and client data is only readable by that salon's own owner and staff accounts. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Booking history is retained for statistical and business reporting purposes. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. financial records for tax purposes). Data cached locally on your device for offline use is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you. Statistics shown to salon owners (e.g. revenue reports) are descriptive summaries of their own salon's data, not automated decisions made about any individual.

12. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for users in the European Economic Area), you have the right to: (a) access your personal data; (b) correct inaccurate data; (c) request deletion of your data; (d) restrict or object to processing; (e) data portability — receive your data in a structured format; (f) withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal. To exercise these rights, contact us at quantumdeveloper2026@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (for example, the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, NAIH, if you are in Hungary).

13. Cookies & Analytics

Fade Lab is a native mobile app, not a website, so it does not use browser cookies. We do not currently integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDK, and we do not use tracking for advertising purposes.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

15. Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within the timeframe required by law, and will notify affected users directly where the law requires it or where the risk to you is high.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including as the App's features change. We will notify you of material changes through the App or via email. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the policy was last revised.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at quantumdeveloper2026@gmail.com.
